Privacy Policy

Last updated November 18, 2026

Who uses the tool

Only the Vice Clipz team. The tool has no public sign-up and does not collect data from visitors or viewers.

What we store

When we connect our own TikTok, YouTube, Instagram or Facebook account, the tool stores that account's access token and basic profile info (name and ID) on our own computer, plus the view, like and comment counts of the clips we posted. Nothing is sold or shared with anyone.

This website

This site has no cookies, analytics or trackers.

Removing data

Disconnecting an account deletes its token from the tool. You can also revoke access at any time in that platform's app settings.
